Policy

How White Space Designs collects, uses, and protects your personal information — in line with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

Last updated: August 2026

01

Who We Are

White Space Designs ("WSD", "we", "us") is a web design and AI automation studio based in Southern Africa, operated by Jason Russell. This policy explains what personal information we collect through whitespacedesigns.co.za, why we collect it, and how it's handled.

For any privacy-related question or request, contact studio@whitespacedesigns.co.za.

02

Information We Collect

We only collect what's needed to respond to enquiries and run the site properly:

  • Contact form submissions: name, email address, and any message content you submit.
  • Booking details: if you book a call via Calendly, your name, email, and selected time slot are handled by Calendly under its own privacy policy.
  • Site analytics: basic, aggregated usage data (pages viewed, approximate location, device type) used to understand traffic and improve the site.
  • Lead research data: where WSD is running lead-generation research on behalf of a prospective client engagement, publicly available business information (company name, public contact details) may be gathered — this does not apply to visitors browsing whitespacedesigns.co.za itself.

We do not collect payment card details on this site — invoicing happens directly via email/EFT once a project is agreed.

03

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to enquiries submitted through the contact form or booking link.
  • To prepare quotes, proposals, and project scoping for prospective clients.
  • To understand how visitors use the site so we can improve it.
  • To meet legal or accounting obligations (e.g. invoicing records).
We never sell your information. Personal information submitted through this site is used solely for the purposes above and is not passed to third parties for marketing.
04

POPIA Compliance

White Space Designs processes personal information in accordance with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). We process information only:

  • With your consent (e.g. submitting the contact form or booking a call), or
  • Where necessary to take steps toward a contract at your request (e.g. preparing a quote), or
  • Where necessary to comply with a legal obligation (e.g. financial record-keeping).

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time. You may also lodge a complaint with South Africa's Information Regulator (inforegulator.org.za) if you believe your information has been mishandled.

05

Third-Party Services

This site relies on a small number of trusted third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

Service Purpose
Calendly Call booking and scheduling
Google Workspace Email correspondence and internal record-keeping
Google Sheets Internal lead and enquiry tracking (WSD staff access only)
GitHub Pages Website hosting

None of these providers are permitted to use your information for their own marketing purposes on our behalf.

06

Cookies

This site uses minimal, functional cookies only — for example, to remember basic display preferences (like currency). We do not use invasive tracking or third-party advertising cookies. You can disable cookies via your browser settings at any time without affecting core site functionality.

07

Data Retention

Enquiry and contact form data is retained only for as long as needed to respond to your enquiry and, if a project proceeds, for the duration of the client relationship plus any period required for accounting records under South African law. You can request deletion of your data at any point where we're not legally required to retain it.

08

Your Rights

  • Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or outdated information.
  • Request deletion of your information, subject to legal record-keeping requirements.
  • Withdraw consent for us to contact you at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, email studio@whitespacedesigns.co.za — we'll respond within a reasonable timeframe.

Questions about this policy?

Reach out any time — we're happy to explain how your information is handled before or after you get in touch.
