Common Questions

Does this replace my front desk?

No — it handles the after-hours and repetitive parts (booking questions, confirmations, reminders) so your team spends their time on clients who are actually in the clinic, not answering the same WhatsApp question for the fifth time that day.

What if a question needs a real answer — pricing for a custom treatment plan, a medical question?

The agent is built to recognise what it shouldn't answer and hand off to a real person, rather than guessing. You decide where that line sits.

Typically 2-3 weeks from kickoff, depending on how much of your service list, pricing, and booking rules need to be documented first — see how the full process works in our onboarding flow.
