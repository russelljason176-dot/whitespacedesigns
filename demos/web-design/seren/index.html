01

Rooibos

Aspalathus linearis

Harvested in the Cederberg mountains, rooibos contains aspalathin, an antioxidant found nowhere else on earth. It neutralises free radicals while supporting the skin's natural collagen network.

02

Baobab

Adansonia digitata

Cold-pressed from the seeds of Africa's tree of life, baobab oil is rich in omega 3, 6, and 9 fatty acids. It repairs the lipid barrier and locks in moisture without heaviness.
