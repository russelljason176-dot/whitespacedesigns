How to Use Seren

Your Ritual

Three steps in the morning. Four at night. That's the complete routine. Every product serves a specific purpose in the sequence.

AM · Morning Ritual

Prepare your skin for the day, and protect what you're rebuilding.

The morning routine is about three things: clean, treat, protect. Skip any of the three and you're undoing the work you did the night before. It takes four minutes.

01

Cleanse

Wash with lukewarm water, not hot. Hot water strips your barrier faster than anything else in a morning routine. Use the Cape Aloe Soothing Gel or Buchu Clarifying Cleanser depending on your skin type. Massage for 45 seconds, rinse, pat dry. Don't rub.

Tip: If you cleansed properly the night before, a short 30-second cleanse in the morning is enough.

Cape Aloe Gel
02

Serum

Apply the Rooibos Renewal Serum to damp skin, not dry. Damp skin allows actives to penetrate more evenly. Three to four drops pressed (not rubbed) across forehead, cheeks, and chin. Give it 90 seconds to absorb before the next step. If you're targeting the eye area, apply Rooibos Eye Repair Serum here too.

Tip: Pressing is more effective than rubbing. Your fingertips warm the product and push it in; rubbing just moves it around.

Rooibos Serum
03

Moisturise & Seal

Finish with the Baobab Deep Moisture Cream. This locks in the serum and forms the day's moisture foundation. Two pea-sized amounts is enough for the full face and neck. If you want extra radiance, mix one drop of Marula Glow Face Oil into the moisturiser before applying.

Tip: The Marula oil blend trick works because oil mixed into a water-based moisturiser distributes more evenly than applying oil alone.

Baobab Cream

PM · Evening Ritual

This is when your skin actually repairs. Give it something to work with.

Skin cell turnover peaks between 11pm and 4am. The evening routine is about removing the day's damage and loading your skin with what it needs for overnight recovery. Five minutes. Four steps.

01

Double Cleanse

First cleanse removes sunscreen and makeup. Second cleanse treats the skin. Start with Cape Aloe Soothing Gel or a dedicated oil cleanser for the first pass, then use Buchu Clarifying Cleanser for the second. Take your time. Sixty seconds per pass, minimum.

Tip: If you skipped SPF today, a single cleanse with the Buchu is sufficient.

Buchu Cleanser
02

Treatment Serum

Apply Rooibos Renewal Serum to damp skin. At night, use four to five drops. The skin is in repair mode and can absorb more. The aspalathin in rooibos is most active when skin temperature drops slightly in the evening. Let it absorb for two minutes.

Tip: Eye repair serums go on before face serums. Thinner formulas first, thicker second.

Rooibos Serum
03

Face Oil (Optional)

If your skin is dry or you're in a Highveld winter, add two drops of Marula Glow Face Oil before your moisturiser. Press it in. Don't rub. This step is optional for oily or combination skin.

Tip: Oil before moisturiser at night. Moisturiser before oil in the morning. The order is different because the goal is different.

Marula Face Oil
04

Night Balm

Finish with Baobab Night Recovery Balm. About half a teaspoon, warmed between your palms and pressed onto the face and neck. This is the occlusive layer that prevents transepidermal water loss while you sleep. It's the step most people skip. Don't.

Tip: The balm works by keeping moisture in, not adding moisture. The serum does the adding. The balm does the locking.

Night Balm

Personalise Your Routine

Find your starting point

Normal / Balanced

Your skin behaves. No major dryness, no excess oil. The full routine works as written, AM and PM without modifications.

Core products

Buchu Clarifying Cleanser Rooibos Renewal Serum Baobab Deep Moisture Cream

Dry / Dehydrated

You feel tightness after cleansing. Prioritise the Baobab products and add Marula oil at both AM and PM. The Night Balm is non-negotiable.

Core products

Cape Aloe Soothing Gel Rooibos Renewal Serum Baobab Night Recovery Balm Marula Glow Face Oil

Oily / Combination

Skip the face oil unless it's winter or you're in a dry climate. Buchu cleanser twice daily. Lighter application of the moisture cream.

Core products

Buchu Clarifying Cleanser Rooibos Renewal Serum Marula Radiance Booster (AM only)
