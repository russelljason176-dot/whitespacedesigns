Our Story

Built from
frustration.
Refined by science.

We didn't set out to build a skincare brand. We set out to solve a problem nobody was solving honestly.

01 / The Problem

Twelve products. Zero results. We knew there was a better way.

It started with a bathroom shelf full of serums, creams, mists, and masks. Skin getting worse by the month. Every product claimed to do everything. Most did nothing specific.

The issue wasn't effort. It was noise. The skincare industry had turned complexity into a marketing strategy. More ingredients meant more perceived value. More products meant more spend. Nobody was asking: what does your skin actually need?

So we started asking. And the answer, again and again, was: less. But better.

02 / The Research

Three years in the Cederberg, one question: what actually works?

We spent three years working with cosmetic chemists, ethnobotanists, and farmers across South Africa's biodiversity hotspots. The goal was proven actives that could justify their place in a formula. Not exotic ingredients to put on a label.

The Cederberg gave us rooibos. Limpopo gave us baobab. KwaZulu-Natal gave us marula. The Cape Winelands gave us buchu and Cape aloe. Five regions. Four plants. Each one with decades of documented skin benefit data that most brands were ignoring.

The formulations took fourteen months to get right. We had forty-three versions of the serum before we had one we were willing to sell.

03 / The Result

Eight products. That's it. That's the whole range.

We launched with eight products because eight was what the science demanded. Not eight to start. Eight, full stop. A complete routine shouldn't require more than four of them. Every product serves a specific function that nothing else in the range duplicates.

We priced them fairly. The ingredients aren't cheap, so neither is the price. But we don't take the 12× markup that characterises luxury skincare. You're paying for what's in the bottle, not the bottle's address.

The first batch sold out in eleven days. We've been back-ordered six times since. We're not in a rush to fix that by cutting corners on sourcing. Some things are worth waiting for.

What we stand for

Four principles we've never compromised on

Ingredient Integrity

Every ingredient has a proven, specific function. If it can't justify its inclusion, it doesn't make the formula.

Traceable Sourcing

Every batch is traceable to a named farm or cooperative. We know the harvest date, the farmer, and the processing method.

SA First

All active ingredients are South African. We're not importing what we already have in the most biodiverse country on earth.

Honest Pricing

You're paying for the formula, not the brand story. Our margins are fair, not extractive. The price reflects the cost of doing it properly.
