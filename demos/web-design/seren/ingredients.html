The Science of Less

Four plants.
Eight products.
One philosophy.

We don't source ingredients to fill a bottle. We source them because the science demands it. Nothing else does the job.

"If an ingredient doesn't have a specific, proven role in the formula, it doesn't make the cut. That's respect for your skin's chemistry."

The Seren Formulation Standard

Active Botanicals

01

Aspalathus linearis

Rooibos

Cederberg Mountains, Western Cape

Rooibos grows only in the Cederberg. Nowhere else on earth. Its fermented extract contains aspalathin, a flavonoid with documented antioxidant activity that outperforms green tea at equivalent concentrations. We use it at 12% in our serums, more than any other SA skincare brand.

  • Neutralises free-radical damage before it reaches skin cells
  • Reduces visible redness and chronic inflammation markers
  • Supports natural collagen synthesis over time
  • Clinically studied, with published peer review

Found in

Rooibos Renewal Serum Rooibos Eye Repair Serum

02

Adansonia digitata

Baobab

Limpopo Province, Northern SA

Cold-pressed from seeds harvested by community cooperatives in Limpopo. Baobab oil has a fatty acid profile (omega 3, 6, 9) that closely mirrors your skin's natural lipid layer, which is why it absorbs without the heavy residue most face oils leave behind.

  • Repairs compromised moisture barriers in 4–6 weeks
  • Omega-rich profile absorbs without occlusive residue
  • High vitamin E concentration (tocopherols) preserves cell integrity
  • Community fair-trade sourced, traceable to cooperative

Found in

Baobab Deep Moisture Cream Baobab Night Recovery Balm

03

Sclerocarya birrea

Marula

KwaZulu-Natal & Mpumalanga

Marula oil is structurally similar to sebum, the oil your skin already produces. That's why it absorbs in under 60 seconds. Our cold-pressing process preserves the oleic acid content at over 70%, which is what gives it the radiance effect without clogging.

  • Mirrors your skin's sebum. Absorbs in under 60 seconds.
  • 70%+ oleic acid content seals moisture without pore interference
  • Antioxidant stability higher than argan oil in independent tests
  • Visibly brightens in 3 weeks with consistent AM use

Found in

Marula Glow Face Oil Marula Radiance Booster

04

Agathosma betulina · Aloe ferox

Buchu & Cape Aloe

Cape Winelands & Eastern Cape

Two plants, one purpose: calm reactive skin without numbing it. Buchu is used traditionally to treat inflammation and infection. Cape Aloe (Aloe ferox, not the supermarket variety) has 200× the antioxidant content of Aloe vera and a documented ability to balance the skin microbiome.

  • Buchu naturally inhibits bacteria associated with breakouts
  • Cape Aloe rebalances skin microbiome diversity
  • Both maintain pH balance, critical for barrier integrity
  • Zero artificial fragrance or masking agents required

Found in

Buchu Clarifying Cleanser Cape Aloe Soothing Gel
