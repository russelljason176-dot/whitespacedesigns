Help

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers. If yours is not here, write to us.

Are your products suitable for sensitive skin?

Every formula is fragrance-free and built around a short list of botanicals. Cape Aloe Soothing Gel is designed specifically for sensitive and reactive skin. If you are unsure, patch test on your inner arm for 24 hours before first use.

How long until I see results?

Hydration and comfort show up within days. For serums with actives, such as the Rooibos Renewal Serum, give it six to eight weeks of consistent use.

Which products should I start with?

Our four-minute routine is a good start: Buchu Clarifying Cleanser, Rooibos Renewal Serum and Baobab Deep Moisture Cream. Add the rest one at a time.

Are your products tested on animals?

No. Seren does not test on animals and neither do our suppliers.

Where do your ingredients come from?

Rooibos comes from cooperatives in the Cederberg, and baobab and marula are sourced from cooperatives in the north of South Africa. Read the story in our journal.

How much is shipping, and how long does it take?

Shipping is R75, and free on orders over R750. Orders ship within 2 working days and usually arrive in 2 to 5 working days within South Africa.

What is your returns policy?

If you are not happy, return unopened products within 30 days for a refund. If something arrives damaged, contact us and we will replace it.

How do you handle my personal information?

We only collect what we need to process your order and reply to your messages, and we never sell your data.
